Glossary

Every term, in a sentence.

The words this site uses, in the sense the two builds use them. Where the builds differ, both senses are given.

Copernicus Sentinel-2, 13 July 2026 · sources

The model

Digital twin

A working model of a real place, kept in software, built from what the place has published about itself, with a clock so the day can run.

Tick

One step of the clock. Ten simulated minutes in the first build; one island minute in the second, 1,440 to a day.

Seed and determinism

A seed is the starting number every random choice grows from; deterministic means the same seed gives the same island on anyone's machine, which is what makes a result checkable.

Headless

Running the whole simulation with no picture, in a terminal, which is how a simulated year is run and compared in minutes.

System

A part that runs every tick and publishes what it knows for others to read: the tide, the ferry, the koalas, the council.

Render layer

One kind of thing drawn on screen: terrain, ocean, sky, trees, buildings, roads, people, vehicles.

Board, or panel

A panel that opens over the island and shows what a system knows. Boards read; they do not change the simulation directly.

Info view

One kind of data painted over the ground in colour, from land cover to who is home tonight. Twenty-two in the first build.

Heightfield

A grid of height values, one per cell, from which the ground is drawn. The first build's is 908 by 1314 cells at 32 metres.

Read model

The summary a system publishes for the boards to show. Some in the first build describe the instant rather than the day.

Lever

One thing a council or another body could decide to do, with what it costs, how long it takes and who decides it. Sixty-two in the first build's civic board.

In tray

The first build's council process: studies to commission, a lodge-it-anyway path, consultations and petitions.

The registers

Real, or known

It exists, somebody published it, and the record names where.

The first build says real; the second says known.

Modelled

The twin worked it out; nobody measured it.

An argument, not an observation, and the panel says what it rests on.

Proposed

Somebody has suggested it and it does not exist.

It stays marked proposed in every panel.

Not known

No fresh source can answer.

The second build prints "contested or unknown" with the date it last looked; the first build's Today board prints "not known" for air and wind when the clock is live and no reading is in hand.

Unconfirmed

A real thing nobody has been able to check lately. Thirty-one businesses carried it in the first build at 10 August 2026.

Contributed

Supplied by a contributor and not yet verified by a second source.

Held

Below the confidence threshold and therefore not shown at all. A normal state, not a warning.

Register

The kind of claim a value is: real, modelled, proposed, or, in the second build, known, modelled, proposed, contributed.

Confidence

How much weight a record holds: high, medium or low in the first build; high, medium, low, contested or unknown in the second.

Where a fact comes from

Source

Where a record came from. In the second build a source is an id in a sources file, never free text, and the file carries the licence as read.

Locator

Where in the source: a page and line, a row in a table, a sailing in a timetable.

Provenance

The whole trail: source, locator, when it was true, when it was believed, how sure, and which register.

Two times, or bitemporal

The second build's rule that every fact keeps when it was true of the island and when the twin believed it, so a correction closes an old belief instead of overwriting it.

Pack

A data file of records, one subject per file, committed to the repository and loaded by the twin. It never needs a network.

Feed

A synced file from a real source with a freshness date, kept beside the packs in the first build and read at start-up only if it is marked for reading.

Connector

A tool that reads one named real source and writes a feed or a pack, run on purpose from the terminal, with a stated cadence and a statement of what it can and cannot know.

Sync

One deliberate run of a connector. The running twin never syncs by itself.

Receipt

The second build's record of a fetch: address, time, status, size, a hash of the bytes, how long it took, the licence as read.

Freshness

How old a reading is against the window its source states. The first build uses four words only: fresh, ageing, stale, unknown.

Source ladder, and rung

Four rungs, best first: instrument, observation, model, simulation. The best fresh rung answers a question; a stale reading drops a rung; the bottom rung never wins in live mode.

Instrument, observation, model, simulation

The four rungs: a reading from a device on the island; an official measurement elsewhere; a calculation from known inputs; the twin's own made-up weather.

Coarsening

Rounding a location before it is committed, so a rescue or a letting is placed within a hundred or two hundred metres and never at the door.

Checking

Gate

A program that reads every file in a repository and refuses the build when a rule is broken. The first build's checks its packs; the second's has fourteen rules over everything.

Instrument

In the second build, a checking tool: headless, determinism, bench, shot, the interface probe, the gate, the board, the self-test.

Fault

A deliberate break switched on in a copy of the build, so an instrument can be proven to notice it.

Self-test

The tool that runs every fault against every instrument, healthy and broken, and writes the matrix. 33 faults against 21 instrument columns on 20 August 2026.

Jurisdiction matrix

The generated table of which instrument catches which fault, and which faults nothing catches.

Board, the noticeboard

Where findings are posted, one note per file, in both builds. A finding that lives only in a chat is treated as never made.

Draw call, and frame

A draw call is one instruction to the graphics chip; a frame is one drawn picture. The second build reports the worst frame, not the average.

The island's own words

Barge, ferry, Flyer

The vehicle barge carries cars and the people in them; the two passenger ferries carry people on foot. SeaLink runs the barge and one ferry; the Gold Cats Flyer is the other.

Landing point

Where a boat lands on the island: the Junner Street ferry terminal, or the One Mile jetty, both at Dunwich.

GTFS

The General Transit Feed Specification, the standard file format TransLink publishes bus timetables in.

Standard port, and harmonic constituents

A standard port is the place a tide table is published for; constituents are the regular swings of known period added together to predict the tide there. Dunwich is the standard port; the first build models seven constituents, the second holds none.

ODbL

The Open Database Licence, which OpenStreetMap data carries. Its share-alike condition is the open decision keeping four packs out of the second build's committed data.

CC BY

Creative Commons Attribution, the licence TransLink and the Queensland Government publish much of their data under: free to use, with credit.
