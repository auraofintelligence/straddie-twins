Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country. Both twins acknowledge the Quandamooka People as the Traditional Owners of Minjerribah and of the lands and waters of Moreton Bay, and pay respect to Elders past and present. Both are built from public records. Neither includes cultural material.

This site

Nothing here changes the twins

  • Nothing here is written back into either twin's code. The twins are read, described and linked.
  • Every screenshot came off the running builds on 2 September 2026. None were touched up.
  • Every count comes from one list of parts in this site's files. No number was typed in by hand.

  • Australian English throughout.

Where the pictures came from

Every island picture is satellite data

The moving island on the front page and the pictures behind each heading come from the same free satellite data. Nothing was drawn by hand.

Colour

Copernicus Sentinel-2

Scene S2C_56JNQ, 13 July 2026, cloud at 0.02 per cent. One pixel covers 10 metres of ground, half a cricket pitch. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026, processed by the European Space Agency. Free to use with credit.

Elevation

Copernicus DEM GLO-30

A height map of the whole world, one reading every 30 metres, made for the European Space Agency. It puts Minjerribah's high point at 221 metres. The first build's README says 233. Another of its files says 239. A 30 metre grid rounds peaks off. On the front page the heights are stretched five times, or the island reads flat across 45 kilometres. Stretch it more and a sand island looks like a mountain range.

Processing

What was done to it

Both layers were cut to one grid: 1152 by 2644 pixels, 17.1 metres each. That covers 19.7 by 45.2 kilometres, a box with the whole island inside it. The July scene came out dark, so it was brightened and the colour strengthened. No colour was invented and nothing was painted in.

The front page turns the heights into a solid shape in your browser and drapes the satellite colour over it. The three townships sit at their real coordinates. A browser that cannot do that gets the same data as a flat picture.
