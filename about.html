About this site

Who built what, and how to correct it.

Two twins, one companion site, three short builds. This page says who made each, what this site does with the twins, what you may do with it, and where to go when something on it is wrong.

The builds

Three builds, two makers.

A digital twin is a working model of a real place that runs on a computer and can say where each of its numbers came from. Two have been built for Minjerribah, and this site explains them.

Minjerribah Living Twin

Built by Luke Catalyst Nathan Hayes with Claude Opus 5, working as an agent swarm: many AI sessions running side by side, each on its own task, sharing one repository. July to August 2026.

Second build

second-island

Built by Luke with Claude, August 2026. Started clean on 18 August 2026 from the lessons of the first build, which keeps running as the reference.

This site

The Straddie Twins

Built by Luke with Claude, September 2026. A public companion that explains the two twins part by part, for anyone arriving cold.

Country

Quandamooka Country.

Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country. Both twins acknowledge the Quandamooka People as the Traditional Owners of Minjerribah and of the lands and waters of Moreton Bay, and pay respect to Elders past and present. They are simulations built from public records and carry no cultural material.

This site

It explains the twins and does not change them.

  • Nothing on this site is written into either twin's repository. The twins are read, described and linked, and left as they are.
  • Every screenshot was captured from the running builds on 2 September 2026, with nothing altered.
  • Every count on these pages is computed from one list of parts, kept in the site's own files, so a number on screen is that list and not a figure typed elsewhere.
  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no framework, no tracking, and no network calls beyond the fonts stylesheet.
  • Australian English throughout.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, say so.
