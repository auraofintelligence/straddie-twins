Two working models of one island

The Straddie Twins are two models of Minjerribah that run in a web browser. They use public records: tides, boats, roads, the fresh water under the sand. They test whether the island gets through a bad day.

The short version

The ladder starts at the island

A ladder runs from the Gumpi jetty to a civilisation that keeps the lights on through anything. The plan calls that joyful responsible abundance.

A twin starts with what the island publishes. Ferry timetable, tide tables, roads, council decisions. The freshwater lens, the rain water sitting in the sand. Put a clock on it. Run a day. Then break it.

Fly around the first build: residents, whales, a barge, a council in tray. The second build started with the numbers. It answers one question: can a car get off the island today?

Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country. Both twins acknowledge the Quandamooka People as the Traditional Owners of Minjerribah and of the lands and waters of Moreton Bay, and pay respect to Elders past and present. Both are built from public records only, with no cultural material.
