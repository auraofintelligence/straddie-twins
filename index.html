Two working models of one island, explained.

The Straddie Twins are two digital twins of Minjerribah that run in a web browser from public records: the tide, the boats, the roads, the businesses, the council levers, the people. This site walks you through both, part by part, with nothing assumed.

In one screen

A twin is a model that can tell you where every number came from.

Take the island. Write down what it has published about itself: the ferry timetable, the road network, the business directory, the tide tables, the council's own list of decisions. Put a clock on it. Let the day run. That is a twin. It is not the island and it does not pretend to be; it is a place to ask questions and see which answers hold weight.

Two have been built for Minjerribah. The first is a living world you can fly around, with residents, whales, a barge and a council in tray. The second started again from the instruments up, and today it answers one question very well: can I get off the island, and can my car.
